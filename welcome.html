SOLOS: Dream Journal

A multivariate experience throughout the day that keeps you in your dreams.

The Eight Parts
Speak your dream.
Reflect about your dream.
Someone else reads your dream aloud.
Listen to your dream that someone else has spoken.
Add to your dream once you listen back.
Speak someone else's dream.
Share a dream prompt from a bank.
Listen to dream instruction.

Every day you will speak your dream when you wake up. Every day you will listen back. Every day you will listen for instruction. All the others occur over time.

Knowing this is just the first step. My question for you is this: how will you approach this new habit?

Your Day

You are here. Tomorrow, when you wake up, your first activity will be to listen to your dreams. Though it is possible to listen to a couple of other people's dreams as well. Those will be waiting for you.

What happens when you connect with this great habit of dreaming?

What happens when you disconnect from your ego, your voice, and your words?

What happens when you let the dream take on a life of its own?
